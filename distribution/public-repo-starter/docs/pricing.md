# Pricing Overview

LeadsMCP is typically sold in one of three ways:

## 1. Private Server License
Best for technical operators who want to self-host.

## 2. Managed Setup Service
Best for founders or agencies who want deployment and onboarding included.

## 3. Enterprise or White-Glove Delivery
Best for multi-client agencies or internal RevOps teams.

Depending on the agreement, usage billing may also be layered on top of the server license for qualified exports.
